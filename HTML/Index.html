About Me

Hello! My name is Tetiana, and this is the story of how some clicks turned into the start of a new tech career. No plan. No fear (well, almost). Just clicks.

Honestly, I had no clue. I didn't really know what I was doing. I thought I'd just check things out, but then I kept going.

Every time I found an answer, there were two or three more questions waiting. And weirdly, that didn't stop me. It made me want to figure out more.

QA didn't happen because I had a big strategy. It just happened. Because I clicked anyway.
